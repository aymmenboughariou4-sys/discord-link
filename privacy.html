Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains what happens with your information when you visit this website or join the LazY Discord server.

This website

This site is a simple static landing page. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't run analytics or tracking scripts, and doesn't collect any personal information from visitors. The only thing it does is link out to Discord when you click "Join the server" or a wallpaper preview.

Images and media

Wallpaper, icon, gif and banner previews shown on this site are hosted externally (for example via Google Drive or an image host) and are loaded directly from those services. Viewing this site may cause your browser to make requests to those third-party hosts, which are subject to their own privacy practices.

The Discord server

Once you click through and join the server, your information is handled by Discord, not by this website. That includes your Discord account details, messages, and activity within the server. Please review Discord's own Privacy Policy for details on how Discord handles your data, and the server's own rules once you're a member for anything specific to how the community is run.

Contact

Questions about this site can be directed to the server owner via the Discord server itself.
